Battle. Absorb. Build.

You're a wizard on an elemental journey, each a branching map of battles, mystery events, shops and healing wells. Summon creatures to fight in turn-based battle. Absorb and add your enemies to your roster of fighters. Collect relics and potions, land your signature moves in short rhythm games, and take on the rival wizard waiting at the end of each act. Max out your high score with challenging modifiers and master the nine elemental forces.

From the maker

Working as a solo developer

Hello.

I’m an environmental scientist with a passion for music and video games. I’ve produced dozens of albums with 50+ musicians across the American West. For seventeen years, I’ve made digital art for the love of it. Lately, I’ve been doing sound design for a local game studio, and I fell in love with the challenge. That’s where this passion project was born.

Cosmic Creatures is the game I always wanted to play growing up. It blends creature collecting with the hook of a roguelike: snappy, exciting, and a real mix of skill and luck. I wanted the magic of a Pokémon-filled childhood in a world with Tolkien’s sense of mystery and adventure. I made the soundtrack too, with a little help from my friends.

It’s free to play. If you love it, one purchase keeps every creature you catch. No ads, no subscriptions, no loot boxes. In a world fighting for your attention, I wanted to make something that took me back to my roots in gaming: simple, complete, ready to play.
